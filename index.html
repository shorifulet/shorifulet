Research

Designing a 2.45 GHz microstrip patch antenna.

IEEE CONFERENCE PAPER · OCT 3–5, 2022

Design of S-Band Microstrip Patch Antenna for Wireless Communication Systems Operating at 2.45 GHz

A low-profile patch antenna on a Rogers RT/Duroid 5880 substrate (εr = 2.2, 1.5 mm, copper patch and ground), designed and simulated in CST Studio Suite. The aim: low return loss, VSWR close to 1, and improved gain, directivity and efficiency for S-band wireless applications. Presented at the 13th International Conference on Computing, Communication and Networking Technologies (ICCCNT), IIT Kharagpur.
